# Torn City Racing Guide (2026)

The published page is `https://vagabond1215.github.io/TORN/#racing`.

## Racing point strategy

Classes are determined by total points **earned in official races**, regardless of spending: E=0, D=25, C=100, B=250, A=475. Awards: 3 points for 1st, 2 for 2nd, 1 for 3rd.

Conservative default: **spend 0 E, 15 D, 63 C, 130 B**. On reaching A, 475 - (0+15+63+130) = **267 points available**. A basic fully upgraded speed-focused Class A car without safety-only parts costs about **266** points. The budgeting widget models the other values and warns for negative balances. The 50-point Racing License is a separate Points Building currency expense, not racing upgrade points.

E: Edomondo Localé (Stålhög 860 alternate), no upgrades. If you insist on one upgrade, Strip Out costs 1 point.
D: Tabata RM2 (formerly Toyota MR2), 15-point limited performance package.
C: Chevalier CZ06 (formerly Corvette Z06; Bavaria Z8 another option), 63 points.
B: Colina Tanprice (formerly Sierra Cosworth) / Echo S4, 130 points; retain Tanprice for Mudpit after Class A.
A: Lolo 458 (formerly Ferrari 458) or Edomondo NSX as generalist, followed by terrain/track specialists.

The current Torn names supersede older guides' real-world brand names.

### Cost-conscious NSX garage (November 2026)

Use **two Edomondo NSX cars**, both Short Ratio / Turbo 3: one **Tarmac (TS3)** and one **Dirt (DS3)**. The tarmac car serves Industrial, Vector, Meltdown, Underdog, Commerce and Sewage. The separate dirt car covers Parkland and is a practical alternative for Hammerhead.

This deliberately replaces the older Tarmac TS2 duplicate. TS2 may be marginally faster on Underdog, Commerce and Sewage, and DS2 may be marginally faster on Hammerhead, but this player's preferred strategy saves racing points and prioritizes two versatile terrain-specific cars. The Two Islands dirt Long Ratio / Turbo 3 specialist remains a later optional upgrade. The table above reflects the **recommended shared-car configuration**, not the theoretical fastest setup for every individual circuit.
